# Publication Readiness

## What V5 can support

- A design action can persistently change the simulated causal organization.
- EFE-guided constitutive intervention can construct, stabilize, and transform a Markov-blanket architecture relative to a diagnostic-only control.
- A full four-role partition can be inferred with a structured posterior rather than independent role marginals.
- Improved causal organization is associated with improved architecture score and viability in the paired benchmark.
- Two partially informed drones can pool structural evidence and enact a group-level boundary that neither can change unilaterally.
- The collective controller outperforms local blankets and an equal-bandwidth communication control on targeted boundary tasks.

## What V5 cannot support

- That a physical drone can autonomously discover an unrestricted state space.
- That Markov blankets alone explain biological or technical individuation.
- That the implemented quadratic gap is a universal path-space metric outside the linear-Gaussian model.
- That every component of the EFE approximation is uniquely derived; several weights remain engineering hyperparameters.
- That the intervention model is rich enough for unmodeled hardware actions; it learns family-level success, not a full nonparametric transition law.
- That the collective blanket proves the unrestricted ontological identity of a swarm; the demonstrated individuation is operational and task-relative.
- That the bilateral handshake or communication graph emerged autonomously; both are engineered experimental institutions.

## Required before submission

- Report the frozen 20-seed benchmark, not the quick run.
- Preserve the diagnostic-only comparison as the primary contrast.
- Present construct, stabilize, and transform separately.
- Report the null gap contrast in transformation as an expected result, not a failure.
- Keep the ontological distinction between `C_t` and `q(M_t)` explicit in every theoretical section.
- Release code, seeds, mission-level CSV, and exact environment metadata.
- Preserve the equal-bandwidth and individual-blanket controls in Part II.
- Report random bilateral success honestly and use reconfiguration as the discriminating targeted-design test.

## Current status

The complete two-part manuscript, frozen outputs, ten tests, Overleaf bundle, and GitHub-ready package are present. The submission is suitable as a philosophical-computational proof of concept. Hardware validation, broader seed sweeps, model-misspecification studies, and external code review would strengthen a journal submission but are not prerequisites for presenting the bounded claim.
